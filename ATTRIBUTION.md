## Original work

Every particle effect definition (`runtime/assets/particles/*.particle.json`), the camera
tour animation (`camera-tour.anim.json`), the saber-swing animation
(`saber-swing.anim.json`), and the showcase timeline (`showcase.timeline.json`) are
original work authored for this project. They **emulate the look/technique** of familiar
VFX (fire, smoke, explosions, black holes, lightsabers) built from CC0 Kenney sprites and
engine primitives (mesh particles, TSL bloom) — no third-party VFX asset, sprite sheet, or
gradient was lifted from any tutorial or marketplace.
